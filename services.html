Service 02

Physical Asset Management & Valuation

Asset valuationCondition analysisLifecycle planning

Valuation is one of our strongest capabilities: we value physical assets on the strength of measured condition and risk data, for reporting, insurance and investment decisions, using our own asset management software.

An asset register is only useful if it reflects what's actually happening to the asset. We manage the full lifecycle: inspection, condition analysis and valuation through to preventive maintenance, compliance and eventual decommissioning. Maintenance spend follows measured condition and risk data, reviewed on a set inspection cycle.

The focus is sustainable asset solutions: optimal replacement cycles, wear management, cost-benefit analysis and revenue maximisation. And because condition data feeds the same team's data engineering and GIS work, it doesn't stay in a PDF report.

  • Condition assessment & inspection
  • Asset valuation for reporting & insurance
  • Asset data management
  • Criticality & risk-based maintenance planning
  • Preventive maintenance programme design
  • Compliance & regulatory reporting
  • Decommissioning & disposal planning

Service 04

GIS & Spatial Analytics

Geospatial mappingRisk modellingLocation intelligence

A spreadsheet of assets tells you what you have. A map tells you where risk is concentrated, which sites are exposed to the same hazard, and what's reachable when something fails. We build interactive maps, a geospatial digital inventory and condition-graded mapping your team can make decisions from.

Location-based data feeds financial models, geographic cost variations and environmental impact analysis, while GIS-based risk management brings high-resolution risk modelling, hazard mapping, real-time weather integration and mitigation planning.
