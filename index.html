Industries

Sector expertise across
asset-heavy industries.

Six sectors where infrastructure can’t afford to fail, and where condition data, spatial context and engineering have to work together.

01 / 06
Sector 01

Energy & Utilities

Grid, generation and utility infrastructure that can't tolerate unplanned downtime. Asset condition data and spatial risk modelling matter more here than almost anywhere else.

Sector 02

Transport & Logistics

Road, rail and logistics networks where asset condition and geographic context decide where failure actually costs you money.

Sector 03

Government & Public Infrastructure

Public infrastructure carries political and compliance exposure a private asset doesn't. Defensible condition data and documented SHERQ process matter as much as the engineering itself.

Sector 04

Financial Services

Fintech and financial operations infrastructure where data pipeline reliability and governance are the actual product being delivered.

Sector 05

Mining & Industrial

Industrial and mining assets operating in harsh, spatially dispersed conditions, where condition data and location intelligence decide maintenance priority.

Sector 06

Telecommunications & ICT

Network and tower infrastructure spread across a huge footprint, where spatial context and asset condition data are the only practical way to manage it at scale.
