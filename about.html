Who we are

We combine expertise in technology, engineering and asset management to help organisations increase value and grow sustainably.

Spryar Tech is a leader in physical asset management, technology solutions and advisory services. Our services cover the full asset lifecycle: asset creation, insurance, operation, maintenance, decommissioning and disposal, all supported by advanced software development.

Most firms specialise in one piece: the asset, the data, or the map. Spryar Tech runs all three through a single SHERQ-aligned delivery team, so an infrastructure decision comes with the condition data and spatial context needed to defend it.
