Spryar Tech works with infrastructure, asset and spatial data every day, so we take the information people and clients trust us with seriously. This statement explains what personal information we handle, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Spryar Tech (Pty) Ltd (“Spryar Tech”, “we”, “us”) is a multidisciplinary engineering and data consultancy providing infrastructure engineering, physical asset management, data engineering and GIS & spatial analytics services. We are based at 11th Floor, Sandton City Towers, Cnr 5th St & Rivonia Road, Sandton, South Africa.

For personal information we collect for our own purposes (for example, enquiries and job applications), we are the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need for the purpose at hand:

  • Enquiries and business contacts: your name, company, job title, email address, phone number and the details of your enquiry.
  • Job and graduate applications: your CV and the information in it, such as qualifications, work history, professional registrations (for example with ECSA) and references you choose to share.
  • Client and supplier relationships: the names and contact details of people we work with at client, partner and supplier organisations, and records needed for contracts, invoicing and compliance.
  • Site visits: when we carry out inspections or attend sites, we may keep attendance and safety (SHERQ) records, and photographs taken for condition assessment. We take care to avoid capturing identifiable people in survey imagery where we can.
  • Website use: limited technical information, as described in section 8.

We do not intentionally collect special personal information (such as health, religious or biometric information) or information about children. If an application includes information like this, we only use it where the law allows and you have consented.

3. How we use it

We use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and prepare proposals;
  • deliver, manage and invoice our services, and meet our contractual obligations;
  • assess job and graduate applications, and contact candidates;
  • keep SHERQ, project and compliance records that we are legally or contractually required to keep;
  • keep our systems and this website secure; and
  • send you industry updates, but only if you have asked us to or have an existing business relationship with us. You can opt out at any time.

We process personal information on the lawful grounds POPIA allows, including your consent, the performance of a contract, compliance with the law, and our legitimate interests in running our business. We do not sell personal information.

4. Client project data

On engagements, clients often give us access to their asset registers, inspection records, financial and operational data, and spatial (GIS) datasets. Some of this can include personal information, for example property owners, account holders or field personnel linked to an asset.

When we handle this data on a client's behalf, we act as an operator under POPIA. We process it only on the client's documented instructions and for the agreed project, we keep it confidential, and we apply the security measures agreed with the client. Questions about that data are best directed to the client organisation responsible for it, and we will assist them in responding.

5. Sharing & cloud storage

We share personal information only where needed, with:

  • service providers who help us operate, such as email, IT, cloud hosting and data platform providers (including Microsoft, Amazon Web Services and Snowflake), professional advisers and insurers;
  • clients and project partners, where needed to deliver an engagement; and
  • regulators, professional bodies or law enforcement, where the law requires it.

Some cloud providers may store or process information outside South Africa. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient is bound by laws, binding agreements or corporate rules that give a level of protection comparable to POPIA, as required by section 72.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as the law, our contracts or professional obligations require. For example, unsuccessful job applications are kept for up to 12 months (in case a suitable role opens) unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Client project data is returned or securely deleted at the end of an engagement in line with the client agreement.

7. Security

We protect personal information with appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access controls, encryption where appropriate, secure cloud configuration and governed data pipelines, and confidentiality obligations for our team and service providers. If we become aware of a security compromise affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. Cookies & this website

  • Necessary storage: we store your cookie choice in your browser so we don't ask on every visit.
  • Optional analytics: if analytics are enabled on this site, they will only run after you choose “Accept all”. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings.
  • Contact form: the form on this website does not store your details on our servers. It opens your own email app with your message ready to send to info@spryartech.co.za, and we handle it as an email enquiry.
  • Third-party content: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. The map on our contact page only loads from Google Maps if you choose to view it; Google may then set its own cookies under its privacy policy.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct, update or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing;
  • withdraw your consent where we rely on it; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Requests for access to records are handled in line with the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA). We may need to verify your identity before responding. Our PAIA manual is available on request.
